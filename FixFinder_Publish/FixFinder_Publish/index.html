Safety checklist

These are general reminders, not a replacement for the manufacturer's service instructions.

  • Power down the device and disconnect external power before internal work.
  • Follow a guide for the exact model and revision.
  • Use the correct precision driver and keep screws sorted by location.
  • Never puncture, bend, crush or pry directly against a lithium-ion battery.
  • Use suitable non-metal tools for delicate connectors where the guide recommends them.
  • Stop if a part resists, the battery is swollen or damaged, or you are unsure.
  • Practice only on devices you have permission to work on, with qualified adult help when needed.
If a battery is swollen, hot, leaking or damaged, do not continue disassembly. Get qualified help.
